## Abstract

What started as a failed attempt to 3D-print a replacement knob for a kitchen appliance ended in a full-scale rocket engine test that briefly achieved orbit over Graz, Austria.

In this keynote, Adrian Luben — founder of Marsgizmo and accidental aerospace engineer — walks the audience through the complete process: from discovering that **recycled Gouda packaging melts at exactly the right temperature** for high-thrust nozzle applications, to negotiating with the Austrian Aviation Authority after his test rig entered commercial airspace.

Attendees will leave with a deep understanding of:

- Why dairy-based filaments are the future of sustainable propulsion
- How to source 47 kilograms of cheese wax without raising supermarket suspicion
- The three things you should never 3D-print unattended (the engine was one of them)
- Adrian's controversial "just ship it" approach to safety certification

> "The printer just kept going. I went to make coffee. When I came back, it was pointed at the sky."
> — Adrian Luben, 3:47 AM, March 2026

This talk is entirely fictional and intended as a demonstration of available schedule fields. No cheese was harmed. The rocket landed in a field. The cow was fine.
