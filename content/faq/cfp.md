# Call for Papers (CFP)

## What is a CFP?

A Call for Papers (CFP) is an open invitation for speakers to submit talk proposals for our conference. If you have expertise in a topic that would interest our audience, we'd love to hear from you!

## Why would you want to talk at Printed Events?

Speaking at Printed Events is a great chance to reach your audience and share your experience with others. Wether its your hobby project, a business or even pitching your idea to the public – we would love to hear your story.

All our talks are also being recorded by our own production team. Most of the talks are also being live streamed on streaming plattforms like Youtube or Instagram and shared online after the event.

You are free to use your very own reording also for your own marketing purpose afterwards.

## How to Submit

To submit your talk proposal:

1. write down what you want to talk about.
2. send us an email with your proposal to <office@printed-events.com>
3. we will reach out to you after 72 hours.

## Questions?

If you have any questions about the CFP process, feel free to contact us at [office@printed-events.com](office@printed-events.com).
