Oliver "Ollie" Pockett is the Additive Manufacturing Lead at Alloyed's Rapid Alloy Research Centre. He started his journey in 3D printing as a hobbyist, tinkering with desktop machines at home, before transitioning into professional industrial additive manufacturing. Ollie bridges the gap between the maker community and high-end AM, bringing community energy into industrial contexts.
