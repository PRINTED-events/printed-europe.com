Thorsten Seyschab (known as toddeTV) is a computer scientist, Senior Fullstack Software Developer, and conference speaker based in Dresden, Germany. He specialises in web technologies, 3D graphics on the web, and Vue/Nuxt development. A regular speaker at conferences including NuxtNation and Vue.js Nation, he brings his passion for the open web to the 3D printing community.
