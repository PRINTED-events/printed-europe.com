Pierre Bolmer, known as The Swedish Maker, is a woodworker and maker from Sweden who combines traditional craftsmanship with modern digital tools including 3D printing. His work explores what it means to create beyond the constraints of a single machine, blending timber, metal, and printed parts into cohesive, beautiful builds.
