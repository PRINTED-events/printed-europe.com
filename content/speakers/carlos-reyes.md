Carlos Reyes, known as Carlos 3D, is a SOLIDWORKS-certified engineer (CSWP) and content creator based in France. Fueled by curiosity, he creates content in French, Spanish, and English covering CAD, 3D printing, and engineering topics. Carlos believes that 3D printing is a superpower that allows makers and engineers to bring virtually any idea to life.
