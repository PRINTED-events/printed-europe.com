Beverly Tan is the founder of Ecovate 3D in Raleigh, NC – one of the first full-service brick-and-mortar retail stores dedicated entirely to 3D printing. Starting as a hobbyist in 2018, she turned her passion into a community hub that sells printers, filament, and offers repair, maintenance, and education services. Beverly is also a community leader who has used 3D printing for disaster relief, including restoring internet to over 100,000 people after Hurricane Helene.
