Daniel Bettencourt, known as Kaizen 3D Prints, is a textile and fashion designer based in the Pacific Northwest who specializes in 3D printing onto mesh and fabric. He pushes the boundaries of what 3D printing can achieve in fashion and wearable design, creating innovative garments and accessories that blend digital fabrication with textile craftsmanship.
