Teula Bradshaw is the CEO and Executive Director of the Sanjay Mortimer Foundation, the charity she helped establish in memory of Sanjay Mortimer – co-founder of E3D and a pioneer of the 3D printing movement. She leads all operational and fundraising activities of the SMF, working to empower neurodivergent young people in STEM and engineering through community, making, and mentorship.
