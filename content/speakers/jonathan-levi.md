Jonathan Levi is a bestselling author, serial entrepreneur, and host of The Next Layer – a rapidly growing YouTube channel and podcast dedicated to 3D printing, making, and creator culture. After selling his previous business in 2022, Jonathan turned his passion for 3D printing into a full-time creative endeavour and community, featuring respected names in the maker space through in-depth interviews and conversations.
