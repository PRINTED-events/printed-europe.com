Noé Jenn-Treyer is the CEO of Alsanor, a company developing humanoid robots. His team uses 3D printing as a key prototyping and manufacturing tool in the development of advanced robotic systems. Noé bridges the worlds of robotics, AI, and additive manufacturing to bring next-generation humanoid robots to life.
