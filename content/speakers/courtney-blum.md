Courtney Blum is the creator behind FilamentStories, a content platform dedicated to showcasing filaments from around the world. A software developer turned filament enthusiast, Courtney built FilamentStories to fill a gap in accessible, honest filament information – connecting makers with material companies and building community around the materials that make 3D printing possible.
