Lutz Feldmann graduated with a Diploma in Plastics Engineering in 1988 in Darmstadt and has followed the 3D printing industry ever since. Over more than 25 years he held senior positions at SolidWorks and Dassault Systèmes, always closely attached to engineering and design. For the last six years he has been managing channel partners for major 3D printing vendors, specialising in continuous fibre supported composite printing. In his current role as Vice President EMEA at Anisoprint, he is building up a channel ecosystem for the company.
