Paulo Kiefe is the creator of STEMFIE, a free, open-source, 3D-printable construction set toy inspired by his childhood love of MECCANO. He began designing STEMFIE in 2017 and has built it into a globally recognised educational making project – proving that open-source 3D printing can create meaningful, joyful learning experiences for children and adults alike.
