## Shaping the Future of Manufacturing. One layer at a time.

**Irena Heuzeroth** is an award-winning engineer, Senior Trainer, and driving force in Additive Manufacturing (AM) at the **SKZ (Plastics Center) in Würzburg**. With a deep passion for industrial 3D printing and plastics technology, she is actively shaping how professionals and leading companies integrate advanced manufacturing into their real-world production lines.

Through her expertly designed training programs and innovative e-learning modules, Irena empowers engineers, technicians, and businesses to master complex 3D printing technologies. Her unique ability to translate cutting-edge scientific research into practical, accessible industrial applications has made her a highly respected and influential voice in the European AM community.

With a strong focus on **industrial qualification, process optimization, and hands-on application**, Irena demonstrates how digital fabrication can solve tangible manufacturing challenges. Her expertise spans the entire 3D printing process chain—from advanced materials science and design engineering to occupational safety and final part production.

Beyond technical research, Irena is deeply dedicated to education and closing the critical skills gap in the manufacturing sector. She is the mastermind behind the pioneering "Certified Industrial Technician Specializing in Additive Manufacturing" program—a milestone achievement that earned her the prestigious **Formnext AMbassador Award**. Through this program, as well as her active involvement in the SKZ Lab for aspiring young innovators, she is transforming how technical education is delivered to both seasoned professionals and the next generation of makers.

Irena Heuzeroth represents the vital bridge between academic research and industrial reality—blending deep engineering expertise, educational innovation, and a passion for emerging technologies. Her training sessions and talks are highly informative, practical, and filled with actionable takeaways for industry professionals, educators, and companies looking to confidently scale their AM capabilities.

### Key Topics

- **Industrial applications of Additive Manufacturing**
- **Bridging the skills gap:** The future of technical education and e-learning
- **Mastering the 3D printing process chain:** From material to finished part
- **From theory to production:** Integrating AM into established industrial workflows
- **Inspiring the next generation:** Building a passion for engineering and plastics
