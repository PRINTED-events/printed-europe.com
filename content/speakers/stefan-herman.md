## Engineering the Truth. One Test at a Time.

**Stefan Hermann**, widely known as the creator of *CNC Kitchen*, is an **internationally** recognized **Additive Manufacturing Engineer** and **technical innovator** whose data-driven content has set the **gold standard** for 3D printing material science and mechanical testing.

By applying his Master’s degree in **Applied Computational Mechanics** and years of professional experience in **aerospace engineering**, Stefan brings a level of **technical rigor** to the community that is unmatched. His mission is to replace guesswork with **verifiable data**, helping **millions of makers** and professionals worldwide understand the true structural limits of additive manufacturing.

With a relentless focus on **mechanical testing**, **optimization**, and **scientific analysis**, Stefan demonstrates how to transform desktop 3D prints into functional, high-performance parts. From his legendary "breaking point" strength tests to deep dives into filament chemistry and heat-insert optimization, he provides the **engineering insights** necessary to build with confidence.

Beyond his YouTube channel, Stefan is a respected voice in the industry, contributing to the global dialogue through the *Meltzone* podcast and collaborations with leading hardware and material manufacturers. He is dedicated to **bridging the gap** between industrial-grade engineering and the desktop revolution, making complex concepts accessible to everyone.

**Stefan Hermann** represents the analytical heart of the maker movement — blending **scientific methodology**, **practical engineering**, and digital storytelling to inspire innovation through evidence. His talks are **insightful**, evidence-based, and packed with actionable data for anyone looking to push the boundaries of **functional 3D printing**.

- Applying aerospace engineering principles to desktop 3D printing
