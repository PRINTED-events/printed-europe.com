Aleksander Sadowski is the founder of ALSADO and a passionate advocate for open-source software in mechanical engineering. His mission is to make FreeCAD the default choice for professional 3D CAD. He has developed a complete open-source workflow for product development, collaborating with organizations like the VDI (Association of German Engineers) and the German Space Agency (DLR). He is also the author of the _FreeCAD Beginner's Handbook_ and an inventor of an open-source-designed safety screw.
