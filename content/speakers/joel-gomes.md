Joel Gomes, better known as Integza, is a Portuguese mechanical engineer turned content creator with over 1.7 million YouTube subscribers. He is known for his wildly inventive and often chaotic experiments – from 3D-printed jet engines to unconventional contraptions – always pushing the limits of what desktop fabrication can achieve. Joel combines deep engineering knowledge with a love for humor and curiosity.
