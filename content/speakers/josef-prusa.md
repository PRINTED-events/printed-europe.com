Josef Prusa is the founder and CEO of Prusa Research, one of the world's leading 3D printer manufacturers. He started building 3D printers as a student in Prague and grew Prusa Research into a global brand trusted by makers, engineers, and professionals alike. An iconic figure in the open-source 3D printing movement, Josef has contributed countless innovations to the community and continues to shape the future of desktop 3D printing.
