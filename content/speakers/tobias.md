Tobias Teufel is the founder of 3DDruckByTeufel, an independent additive-manufacturing consultancy based in Kaiserslautern, Germany. Since founding the company in early 2021, he has helped companies identify and implement 3D-printing applications, from assembly fixtures to spare parts. He also offers IHK-certified 3D-printing training courses and has spoken at the 3D-Druck Forum Pfalz.
