Werner Fugmann is the creator behind WF3D, a YouTube channel and brand focused on designing and 3D printing anime and gaming-inspired creations. Based in Germany, Werner uses Fusion 360 to sculpt detailed character models and props, blending fan culture with digital fabrication. His work shows how 3D printing can transform pixels into tangible art.
