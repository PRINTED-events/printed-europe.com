## 2. General Information

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This privacy policy explains what data we collect when you visit this website and how we use it.

## 3. Data Collection on This Website

### Server Log Files

When you visit this website, our hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files. This includes:

- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Referrer URL
- Hostname of the requesting device
- Date and time of the request

This data cannot be attributed to specific individuals and is not merged with other data sources. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in the technically error-free operation of the website).

### Cookies

This website may use technically necessary cookies to ensure its proper functioning. These cookies do not store any personally identifiable information. You can configure your browser to block cookies, though this may affect some website functionality.

## 4. Ticket Purchase

Ticket sales are handled via [tito](https://ti.to), a third-party ticketing service. When purchasing a ticket, you provide personal data (name, email address, payment details) directly to tito. Please refer to tito's privacy policy at [https://ti.to/privacy](https://ti.to/privacy) for details on how your data is processed.

## 5. Contact via Email

If you contact us by email, the information you provide (including your email address) will be stored for the purpose of processing your request. This data will not be shared with third parties without your consent. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR.

## 6. Speaker Applications (Call for Papers)

If you apply as a speaker through the form at [/cfp](/cfp), we process the details you enter: your name, email address, creator handle and social media channels, your talk topic and description, your stage experience, your sponsoring situation and anything else you tell us. We use this data only to review your application, to contact you about it and, if your talk is accepted, to plan the event with you. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (steps prior to entering into an agreement at your request).

The form sends your application as an email to our team and a confirmation with a copy of it to the email address you entered. For this we use Resend (Resend, Inc., 2261 Market Street #5039, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA) as a processor under a data processing agreement. The emails are processed in Resend's EU region in Ireland. Details: [https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy). If the form opens your own email program instead, your application is sent through your email provider and is handled as described under "Contact via Email".

We delete applications that are not accepted no later than six months after the conference. Details of accepted speakers are kept as long as needed for the event and its follow-up, and as required by law.

## 7. Newsletter

You can sign up for our newsletter on this website. We ask for your first name and email address and, with your consent, use them to send you news about PRINTED events a few times a year. The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR. We store the time of your sign-up, the page you signed up on and the wording of the consent you gave as proof of your consent.

We send the newsletter with HubSpot (HubSpot Ireland Limited, 1 Sir John Rogerson's Quay, Dublin 2, Ireland; parent company HubSpot, Inc., USA) as a processor under a data processing agreement. When you sign up, our website passes your details directly to HubSpot; no HubSpot script runs in your browser. HubSpot may record whether you open the newsletter and click its links. Transfers to HubSpot, Inc. in the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Details: [https://legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy](https://legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy).

You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every newsletter or by writing to [office@printed-events.com](mailto:office@printed-events.com). We then delete your details from the newsletter list, unless we need to keep them for another purpose.

## 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
- **Rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
- **Erasure** of your data ("right to be forgotten") (Art. 17 GDPR)
- **Restriction** of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- **Data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR)
- **Object** to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [office@printed-events.com](mailto:office@printed-events.com).

## 9. Right to Lodge a Complaint
